HOW LEAFWISE WORKS

Helpful clues.
Honest limits.

An open, self-hostable app built around a bundled ONNX model—not a paid third-party prediction API.

ONNX · 38 CLASSES
01

One clear photo

Choose a single leaf in good light. The web service processes it in memory; history and photo storage happen only if you opt in. The Android app runs on-device.

02

A ranked result

The classifier compares the photo with its 38 supported crop/disease labels. The score helps rank those labels; it is not a calibrated probability.

03

A practical next step

Care notes are bundled offline, link to agricultural sources, and favor cautious actions. Healthy classes receive maintenance advice, not a disease treatment.

!

IMPORTANT LIMITS

Use this as a first look

  • It is not a general plant identifier and has no “unknown plant” class.
  • Field photos, lighting, background, multiple leaves, or unsupported crops can cause mistakes.
  • The reported validation score comes from the source repository and cannot be independently reproduced because its original dataset is not included.
  • Confirm serious or uncertain symptoms with local agricultural extension. Do not apply chemicals based on this result alone.
